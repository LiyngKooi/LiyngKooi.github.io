About

I grew up in a traditional Malaysian Chinese family where being good meant being quiet. I learned early that mistakes were embarrassing, and for years, I swallowed my words out of fear of judgment. That changed during my undergraduate studies in the United States, when a professor noticed my anxiety and told me: "No one cares about your accent. They care about your story." That moment gave me the permission to speak and fundamentally shifted how I view learning, human interaction, and psychological safety.

This personal realization deeply shaped my path. While volunteering at an English camp in Kaohsiung and serving as an online reading mentor for a left-behind child in rural China, I saw firsthand that engagement is not merely a matter of ability. It relies heavily on emotional support. When children feel seen and encouraged, they take risks. This taught me that any environment, physical or digital, must be designed to reduce fear and foster trust.

Today, I bridge this human-centric empathy with data-driven methods. Building on my Master's degree in Management Science and Engineering from Zhejiang University, I apply natural language processing and time-series modeling to understand how emotional environments and communication strategies influence human decision-making. Simultaneously, as a Project Lead under the NKNU TEEP program, I build interactive WebXR experiences that prioritize cultural respect and user comfort.

Whether I am investigating how people build resilience, analyzing behavioral data, or developing new technological solutions, my core motivation remains the same. I want to leverage both data and empathy to create environments where people feel secure, understood, and empowered to grow.
